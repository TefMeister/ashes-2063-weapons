# Big guns knock enemies backwards, and kills send them flying

Order: 9
From: mod-ideas `games/ashes-2063.md` (<https://github.com/TefMeister/mod-ideas/blob/main/games/ashes-2063.md>), copied 2026-09-23

`[raw]` · `[looks doable]` — *not tried*

> "make shotguns, sniper rifle and other weapons that make sense to knock the enemy back, send
> enemies flying bacwards when they get killed"

A shotgun or rifle hit shoves the enemy back instead of leaving them standing in place, and a
killing blow throws the body away from you.

**What it'd take:** this is the cheap one. GZDoom already pushes things when they are hit; the mod
would set a per-weapon shove strength and add a stronger one on the death hit. No new artwork at
all. Expect a tuning pass — too much and it looks comic, too little and you cannot feel it — and
heavy enemies will want to resist it or the game gets easy.

⚠️ Verdict unchecked against the real game.
