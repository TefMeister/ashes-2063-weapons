> "make floating hands for the game."

Hands that hover in the air where your controllers are, with no arms attached — the standard VR
look. Today there is a gun and nothing holding it.

**What it'd take:** the engine side is already done, because mods can now see both controllers (see
[The left hand is recognised in the game](#the-left-hand-is-recognised-in-the-game)). What is
missing is **art**: a pair of hand models in the game's chunky pixel style, plus poses for gripping,
an open hand and a trigger finger. That is the same modelling job as the weapons, and the bigger
half of the work. Two hands on long guns below wants exactly this too.

⚠️ Not checked in the real game.
