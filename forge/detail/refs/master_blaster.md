# Master Blaster flamethrower: what the references show (2026-11-02)

Pictures: MEGA `3D blender/refs-2026-11-02/flamethrower/` (`_game.png` = the game's own frames enlarged, `_real.png`).

## The game's Master Blaster (NAPALM1-4, pickup LAUPA0)

A black barrel with a mottled, rag-wrapped stretch and a red ring; a silver nozzle that glows at firing; a dark,
curved wooden body; a black block with a cap on top; a big round gauge at the back facing the player (brass rim, white
face, red-yellow-green arc, needle); a red valve wheel; a copper-orange tank underneath; braided hose.

## Real flamethrowers and parts

- **M2 / M9 (US) and ROKS (Soviet):** the wand is rifle-like, with a wooden stock or grips, a valve lever as trigger,
  an igniter housing at the muzzle with a small pilot jet; tanks on a backpack frame, joined by thick hoses with brass
  fittings and hex nuts; filler caps on top of the tanks; pressure valves with wheels.
- **Pressure gauge (WIKA):** black case, brass stem with a hex, a bezel ring, a white face with dense tick marks, two
  scales, a needle with a hub.
- **2-litre PET soda bottle:** five-lobed petal base, ribbed body, a wide label band, a shoulder tapering to the neck,
  a neck ring, a ribbed screw cap.

## What the detailed model takes from each

Game: the overall layout, colours, gauge, valve wheel, red ring, rag wrap, silver nozzle.
Real: igniter box and pilot tube with a little flame, finned nozzle with the jet set back, collar with hex nuts, brass
hose fittings, filler cap with a knurled edge and a chain, gauge ticks/stem/bezel, valve lever trigger, front grip.
Wasteland: one of the two fuel tanks is a used soda bottle (faded peeling label, murky fuel showing), held by two hose
clamps with straps and a band of duct tape; its cap carries the braided hose into the body; rag and wire wraps; rust on
the hoops; soot round the nozzle; a welded patch plate on the body; duct tape on the grip and the red hose; chipped paint
on the black block.
