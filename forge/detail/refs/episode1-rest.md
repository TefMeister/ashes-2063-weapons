# The other nine Episode 1 weapons: what the references show (2026-11-02)

Pictures: MEGA `3D blender/refs-2026-11-02/<gun>/` (`_game.png` = the game's own frames enlarged, `_real.png` /
`_both.png` = photos beside them). Each design file's header lists in full what it takes from the game, from the
photos and from the wasteland layer; this page keeps the notes and the credits in one place.

## 9mm Autoloader (Glock 17 / 19)

Game: Glock-like, dark grey frame, slide worn to red-brown rust and old blood, big squared sights, red-brown mag base.
Real: rear slide serrations, ejection port with the barrel hood, extractor, rolled slide text, slanted slide nose,
recoil spring guide, rail with slots, takedown lever, slide stop lever, two frame pins, square guard with a serrated
hook, safety blade in the trigger, magazine release, finger grooves, square grip texture, logo panel, beavertail.
Credits: Domenjod (CC BY-SA 4.0, two), Steve Dock (OGL v1.0), Shotgun (CC BY-SA 3.0).

## Pump-action shotgun (Remington 870)

Game: dark blued receiver, red shell in the port, ribbed orange pump, pistol grip with blue and black tape.
Real: grooved receiver top, bolt and extractor in the port, loading port with the lifter, trigger plate with two
pins, cross-bolt safety with its red ring, action bar lock, barrel ring and clamp, knurled magazine cap with a sling
loop, brass bead, action bars, corn-cob pump.
Credits: Spc. Robert Farrell (public domain, three), simonov (CC BY-SA 2.0), US Air Force (public domain),
Mortimer452 (CC BY-SA 3.0), Mitch Barrie (CC BY-SA 4.0).

## Sawed-off (boxlock side-by-side)

Game: dark double barrels cut short, top lever, cut-down wooden grip.
Real: matted top rib, bottom rib, brass bead, engraved boxlock action, hinge knuckle, checkered spade top lever,
safety slide, double triggers, guard tang screws, splinter fore-end with its latch, checkering.
Credits: Cjp24 (CC BY-SA 4.0, two: Chapuis Progress, Charlin R10), Oficersky Romans (CC BY 3.0).

## Machine Pistol (Ingram / Cobray M11)

Game: grey-black stamped box receiver, mag through the grip, top cocking knob, long dark suppressor.
Real: threaded barrel stub, front post between guard ears, rear peep sight, cocking slot, port and bolt, selector,
guard with sling loop, receiver pins and rivets, back plate holes, mag base plate and heel release.
Credits: Stephen Z (CC BY-SA 2.0, three).

## Battle Rifle (FN FAL / L1A1)

Game: tan wood furniture on a black receiver, box sight with a round lens and a red sticker, slotted flash hider.
Real: ribbed dust cover, folding carry handle, left charging handle, rear peep, gas block with regulator and front
post between ears, three oval handguard vents, bayonet lug, ribbed 20-round magazine and its release, selector and
takedown lever, lower pivot pin, swivels, butt plate.
Credits: Gungadin1912 (CC BY-SA 4.0, drawing), Esquerroa (CC BY-SA 3.0), Ken Griffiths (CC BY-SA 4.0), AVDKM
(CC BY-SA 4.0), Springfield Armory NHS (public domain).

## JackHammer (petrol breaker)

Game: red finned engine under a silver slotted shroud, yellow label on a black plate, roll cage, taped carry handle,
fuel cap, six-barrel drum in front.
Real: cylinder fins, spark plug with boot and lead, muffler with a holed heat shield, pull-start with a T handle, fuel
cap with breather, choke, air filter cover with its clip, throttle cable, warning stickers.
Credits: Baumeister 99 / Wacker Neuson (CC BY-SA 3.0), sarang (public domain), Anthony Appleyard (CC BY-SA 3.0),
Friedrich Haag (CC BY-SA 4.0).

## Junker Musket (M1903 Springfield)

Game: long home-made rifle, orange-brown wood, brass bands, copper wire, red cloth at the wrist, fat black scope with
white scribbles, slotted muzzle cap.
Real: folded ladder sight, bands with sling and stacking swivels, front sight blade, ball bolt handle, knurled cocking
piece with the safety flag, cut-off lever, floor plate, guard screws, fore-end finger grooves, checkered butt plate
with a trap door, leather sling; scope rings and clamp screws, turrets, eye cup.
Credits: Bouillant Achille (CC BY-SA 4.0, two), Curiosandrelics (CC BY-SA 3.0), Bradley Lanphear (CC BY-SA 4.0),
Quickload (CC BY-SA 3.0).

## Crow-bar

Game: long dark bar, worn red paint, blood, goose-neck with split claw, bent chisel.
Real: hex bar, V nail slot with bevelled tips, sharpened chisel, forging marks, maker's stamp.
Credits: Pearson Scott Foresman (public domain), YVSREDDY (CC BY-SA 3.0), Ll1324 (CC0).
