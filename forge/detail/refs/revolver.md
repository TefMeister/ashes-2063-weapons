# Revolver: what the references show (2026-11-02)

Pictures: MEGA `3D blender/refs-2026-11-02/revolver/` (sheet `_sheet1.png`), plus the game's in-hand frames
(`3D blender/ashes revolver/reference/sheet_game.png`) and Tefa's earlier Taurus photo sheet.

## The game's revolver

Bright, almost chrome steel. Heavy barrel with a full-length under-lug and a vented rib on top. Fat fluted cylinder,
six chambers, brass showing at the back when open. Spur hammer. Orange front-sight insert. Dark rubber finger-groove
grip, held in a black fingerless glove.

## Real revolvers of that build (Taurus 66/627 Tracker, S&W 686)

- **Barrel:** full under-lug enclosing the ejector rod; top rib with long rectangular vent ports (Tracker); ramp front
  sight with a red or orange insert held by a roll pin; recessed muzzle crown; maker's name and calibre rolled into the
  barrel side in big letters.
- **Frame:** side plate on the RIGHT side, its seam a closed curve behind and below the cylinder window, held by three
  screws (top rear, bottom front of the grip frame, under the cylinder front), plus the yoke screw at the front.
  Hammer and trigger pivot pins show on both sides. Serial number near the crane.
- **Cylinder:** six long flutes between the chambers; a cylinder stop notch over each chamber near the back, with a
  short lead-in groove before it; chamfered back edge; the cylinder gap and the forcing cone visible at the front of
  the window, usually black with burnt powder; the ejector rod tip in front of the cylinder; star extractor at the back.
- **Cylinder latch:** a grooved thumb piece on the LEFT side behind the cylinder, held by a screw.
- **Top strap:** fine anti-glare serrations along it; adjustable rear sight with a blade and notch (often a white
  outline), an elevation screw on top and a windage screw on the right side.
- **Hammer:** sits in a slot behind the top strap, its spur checkered.
- **Grip:** rubber finger grooves with stippling, or wood with checkered panels; a gold medallion on each side (Taurus);
  grip screw through from the left; some have a lanyard ring at the butt.
- **Wear:** holster wear shows as fine straight lines along the barrel and lug edges; the cylinder front and forcing
  cone go black; rust starts at screws, in flutes and on the frame's underside.

## What the detailed model takes from each

Game: the chrome-bright steel, vent rib, fat fluted cylinder, orange insert, dark finger-groove grip.
Real: everything in the list above at quarter-cube size.
Wasteland: soot, rust in the flutes and underside, holster wear lines, seven tally marks on the left frame, duct tape
round the lower grip, paracord through the lanyard ring.
